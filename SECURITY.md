# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email <hello@getadology.com> with details of any security issue. We aim to acknowledge reports within 2 business days.

In your report, include:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce
- Affected components (plugin, MCP server, OAuth flow)
- Your name/handle (for credit, if desired)

## Safe harbor

Good-faith security research conducted in accordance with this policy is authorized. Researchers acting in good faith will not be subject to legal action by Adology, provided that:

- You do not access, modify, or destroy data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue
- You do not exploit the issue beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it
- You do not publicly disclose the issue before we have had a reasonable opportunity to address it
- You do not violate the privacy of Adology users

## Scope

In scope:

- This plugin (`content-intelligence`)
- The MCP server at `https://mcp.adologyai.com`
- The OAuth flow via Stytch B2B
- The protected resource metadata endpoint

Out of scope:

- Third-party platforms whose data is indexed (TikTok, Instagram, etc.)
- Adology web app surface unrelated to this plugin

## Disclosure timeline

We will work with you to validate, fix, and disclose verified issues. Critical vulnerabilities are typically addressed within 30 days; lower-severity issues within 90 days. We will credit you in release notes if you wish.
