Verify Externally Checklist

Content intelligence is one layer. Verify these through external means.
  • Audience demographics confirmationRun candidates through a third-party panel (Comscore, Resonate). Lifestyle labels are directional, not verified demographics.
  • Existing partnership exclusivitiesConfirm none of the Tier 1 brands have an active or pipeline partnership in the focal's category.
  • Proprietary card / payment-rail conflictsFor fintech focals: check Target RedCard, Walmart Pay, or other card relationships before pitching.
  • Partnership-team responsivenessTwo-week SLA on first conversation is the working benchmark.
  • Compliance review for embedded utilityAny embedded payment / cash-flow integration requires legal review on money-movement licensing per state.
  • Co-marketing budget alignmentDifferent partners operate on different commercial models (media-fee, rev-share, etc.). Align before the pitch.
  • Re-run scoring once missing feeds populateIf any brand or competitor feeds were empty at scoring time, backfill and re-score before final BD decision.
